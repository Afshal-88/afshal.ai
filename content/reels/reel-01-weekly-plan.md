> One prompt plans a full week of posts for any business: reels, carousels and stories, each with a hook, a script and a caption.
>
> You fill in 3 things: your business, your customer and your offer. Then you edit what doesn't sound like you.

## The prompt (send this to everyone who comments PLAN)

```
You are a social media strategist for a small business.

Business: [what you sell and where]
Customer: [who buys it and what they care about]
Offer this week: [one thing you want to push, or "none"]
Language: [English / Urdu / Roman Urdu]

Plan 7 days of Instagram content, Monday to Sunday.
Mix: 3 Reels, 2 carousels, 2 story sets.

For each day give:
1. Format
2. Hook: the first line, under 10 words, no greeting
3. Script or slide text: short, plain spoken language
4. Caption: 2–4 lines with one call to action
5. What to film or design, in one line

Rules:
- One idea per post.
- Mention the offer in at most 2 of the 7 days.
- No made-up numbers, reviews or results.
- Write like the owner talks, not like an ad.
```
