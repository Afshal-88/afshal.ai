01
Plan the edit
Turn your script or raw clip into a cut-by-cut edit plan.
Edit plan
Any AI
Copy prompt
Here is my script or the transcript of my raw video: [PASTE]. Target: [PLATFORM], [LENGTH] seconds, for [AUDIENCE].
Make an edit plan as a table: time, what is said, what is on screen (A-roll, b-roll, text, zoom), and the reason. Cut anything that doesn't move the story. Aim for a visual change every 2–3 seconds.
02
Fix the first 3 seconds
Most people decide in the first moment. Give them a reason to stay.
Hook options
Same chat
Copy prompt
Write 6 opening hooks for this video, each under 10 words, in different styles: question, bold claim, mistake, result first, curiosity, pattern interrupt. For each, describe the first frame on screen and the text overlay. Mark the 2 strongest and say why.
03
Captions that read well
Captions keep silent viewers watching.
Caption script
Same chat
Copy prompt
Split my script into on-screen captions: max 4 words per line, one idea per caption, key words marked in CAPS for highlight colour. Add emoji only where they help meaning. Give me the list in order so I can paste it into CapCut auto-captions and fix the timing.
