Write the brand brief

Brand brief
Any AI
Copy prompt
Interview me to write a brand brief. Ask one question at a time, up to 10: what we sell, who buys, why they'd pick us, competitors, price level, personality, values, where people will see the brand, what we never want to look like. Then write the brief on one page.
02
Name ideas
Names that are easy to say, spell and find.
Brand names
Same chat
Copy prompt
Suggest 20 brand names from the brief: mix real words, compound words, invented words and Urdu/English blends if it fits. For each: meaning, why it fits, and a .com or .pk style domain idea. Tell me to check trademarks and Instagram handles myself before choosing.
03
Brand voice
How the brand talks, so every post sounds like one person.
Voice guide
Same chat
Copy prompt
Write a brand voice guide: 3 voice words with what they mean and don't mean, words we use and never use, how we write a caption, a reply to a complaint and a sales message. Give one example of each in English and Roman Urdu.
