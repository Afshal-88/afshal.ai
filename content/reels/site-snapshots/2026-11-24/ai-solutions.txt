Questions
Do I need technical knowledge?
No. The training is written for the person who will run it day to day, not for developers.
What happens after the two months?
You run it yourselves. If you want help later, there is an optional support plan, but you are never locked in.
Who owns the accounts?
You do, from day one. Everything is set up in your name, so nothing stops working when we hand over.
Who pays for tools like the WhatsApp API or ChatGPT?
You pay for them directly from your own account. We tell you the expected tool costs before we start.
Can it work in Urdu?
Yes. Replies and content can be in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.
How much does it cost?
Two monthly payments. The amount depends on what we set up, so we quote it after the free audit.
Do you guarantee more sales?
No. We set up systems that answer faster and follow up more reliably. How much that grows sales depends on your offer and your market.
