01
Know the reader
Good copy starts with what the customer already thinks.
Customer research
Any AI
Copy prompt
I sell [PRODUCT/SERVICE] at [PRICE] to [CUSTOMER]. Here are real reviews, comments or questions from customers: [PASTE].
Give me: their top 5 problems in their own words, what they've tried before, their fears about buying, what "success" looks like to them, and the exact phrases I should reuse. Only use what's in the text I gave you.
02
Find the angle
One clear promise beats a list of features.
Offer and angle
Same chat
Copy prompt
Based on the research above, write 5 different angles to sell my offer (pain, gain, speed, fear of missing out, identity). For each: the one-line promise, who it suits best, and the proof I'd need to make it believable. Pick the strongest and explain why.
03
Write the headlines
Most people only read the headline. Make it count.
Headline set
Same chat
Copy prompt
Write 15 headlines for the chosen angle: 5 short (under 8 words), 5 specific (with a real detail), 5 question-style. No hype words like "revolutionary" or "game-changing". Then pick the 3 best for a website, an ad and a WhatsApp message.
